Made a background, layout, palette or effect with Layout Forge? Share it here.

A useful showcase includes:

- A screenshot or GIF of the result.
- A **Copy link** URL so other people can explore the settings.
- Where you used it: a landing page, app, presentation, experiment or something else.
- One detail you learned or one change that would make the workflow easier.

Try the [mesh gradient generator](https://vumox.github.io/layout-forge/#tools/mesh), [clip-path editor](https://vumox.github.io/layout-forge/#tools/clip) or [glassmorphism generator](https://vumox.github.io/layout-forge/#tools/glass) if you want a starting point.

Links carry settings, not uploaded media. Please avoid putting private content in a public link or screenshot.

The [six tool GIFs](https://github.com/vumox/layout-forge/tree/main/docs/demos) are also available for examples and posts.
