Current priorities and existing issues are in [ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/vumox/layout-forge/blob/main/ROADMAP.md). We are especially interested in canvas accessibility, localization, better exports and practical new generators.

For a reproducible bug, please open a separate bug report. For work you made with the tools, use [Show and tell](https://github.com/vumox/layout-forge/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).

The core stays free, MIT licensed and usable without an account. Suggestions and reactions guide priorities; they are not delivery promises.
